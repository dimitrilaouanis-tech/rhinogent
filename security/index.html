Security and data

Where your data goes, stated once.

Three places: your device, our server, and an outside AI provider only when you choose Pro mode. This page says what is kept in each.

Two ways an answer is made.

ModeWhere your message goesWho makes the answer
StandardOur server, and nowhere else.A model running on our own server.
ProOur server, then an outside AI provider.DeepSeek, Groq, Cerebras or Google. You choose Pro yourself each time.

On your device

The agent's key is made here. If you are signed out, your chats and credit balance stay in your browser.

stays here

On our server

Your email, your chat history, your list of agents and your credit balance. If you set a password, a copy of each key that is encrypted in your browser before it is sent.

stays with us

With an outside provider

The messages you send in Pro mode, so the provider can write the answer. Nothing is sent to them in standard mode.

What we are building next.

  • A second site and off-site backups. So your agent and its record survive any single failure.
  • A signing key for every agent. Today our server signs each record; next, each agent signs its own.
  • An outside security audit. Planned before we take on business clients at scale.
