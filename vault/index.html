Key passphrase · optional

You don't need this to use Rhinogent. Your login, your agents and chat all work without it. A key passphrase is only for having an agent sign with its own private key— set it once if you want that, skip it otherwise. It never leaves your device and we can't reset it.

Checking your account…

Your keys are encrypted on your device before anything is saved. We store only the encrypted blob — never your passphrase, and never a key we could read. That also means we can't recover it for you.
