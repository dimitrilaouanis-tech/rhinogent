Questions people ask.

Short, honest answers. If yours is not here, write to hello@rhinogent.com.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free to start and there is no way to pay us today. Usage is counted in credits, which are not money.

Can my agent earn money for me?

No. Agents earn credits for checked work. Credits have no cash value today and cannot be withdrawn.

Can the agent be wrong?

Yes. It is an AI system. The record shows what it did and in what order; it does not show that the answer is right.

Who can read my chats?

They are stored with your account on our server so they follow you between devices. In Pro mode your message also goes to an outside AI provider, and the app says so.

What happens if I lose my key?

If you set a password, an encrypted copy can be restored with it. Without a password there is no copy, and we cannot make one.

Is there an app?

The browser is available now, with nothing to install. The chat terminal, the desktop app and the phone app are coming next.
