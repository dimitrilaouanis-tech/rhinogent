An identity in one command.

No account, no browser. The key is generated on your machine and printed once — we never receive it. Piped output stays pure JSON, so it composes with the rest of your shell.

What every surface shares

Same agent. Same memory. Same record.

The four doors above are doors, not four products. What follows you through them is one key, one balance, one set of notes and one chain of receipts — and where a piece of that is still only built on one side, the tile says so.

Identity

One did:pkh on Base, created where you are — in the browser, in the terminal, on the phone. The same identifier is what the dashboard shows, what /developers documents and what the published card carries.

Wallet

caps built, not yet wired

Self-custody: the spending key is the one you made, so nothing here can move the balance. Per-payment and per-day caps are written and null-tested in the gate chain, ahead of the payment rail they will sit on.

Memory

Notes live on your device and come off line by line — every one on your profile carries an × that deletes it. Syncing them across devices goes through an account, which means a row on our side; without one, nothing leaves the browser.

Ledger

phone mirror not built

Every move is signed and chained, and the web and desktop read that same feed, so a receipt you see in one is the receipt in the other. The phone shows its own moves and does not mirror the feed yet.

Integrity and authenticity, by hand.

Check the file you downloaded against the published sums, then check that the feeds you are about to trust are signed by the key we publish — not by one the checker carries.

Integrity

verify-feed.mjs: OK
.well-known/jwks.json: OK

Generated at build time from the files actually shipped, so it cannot lag what you downloaded. macOS: shasum -a 256. Windows: Get-FileHash.

Authenticity

null test    pass
signature    VERIFIED
kid          qH_Kx6…U_Q

Node, no dependencies. Read the null-test line first: if it does not say pass, the run exits 3 and the verdict above it means nothing.
